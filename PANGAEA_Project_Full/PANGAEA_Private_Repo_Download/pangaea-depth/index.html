THE DEPTH BUILD

A world with reasons.

People choose work from needs, skills, community shortages and memories. They walk legal routes, gather finite resources, carry supplies home, build, form households and pass knowledge to children.

What is actually running

Deterministic utility AI, bounded episodic memory, rule-based reflection, kinship tracking, seasonal crops, stockpile accounting, 36 discoveries, five transport tiers, industrial production and household relocation. No LLM, cloud account or paid API is required.

Research roots

Generative Agents: importance, recency and relevance in memory retrieval.
Humanoid Agents: needs and social closeness.
GovSim: finite shared resources and sustainability.
Concordia: separate proposed intentions from authoritative world outcomes.

These are original, simplified game implementations inspired by the papers, not ports or reproductions of their research systems. The source package contains the cited research review and integration roadmap.

Honest boundaries

Still a prototype, not AAA production. Local maps are generated from a settlement's seed and biome, not a seamless globe-to-room landscape. Provision-convoy labour is abstracted. No combat, player-written laws, generated dialogue, genetic model or trained neural policy. Large-city frame rates depend on hardware; the laboratory is a synthetic stress fixture. Childhood, family size, technology and seasons use game rules. Version 0.2 saves migrate to the city ruleset; version 0.1 saves cannot be imported.

Controls

Drag to move the view. Scroll to zoom. Click a person or building to inspect. Space pauses; G opens the world; C opens the settlement; F recentres. High speed is a target, limited by the available CPU. The world does not progress after its tab is closed.
