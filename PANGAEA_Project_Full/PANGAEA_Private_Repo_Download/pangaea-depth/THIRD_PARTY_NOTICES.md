# Third-party material

FlatQueue 3.0.0 by Volodymyr Agafonkin is included unchanged in `src/vendor/flatqueue.mjs` under the ISC license. Its complete notice is in `src/vendor/flatqueue.LICENSE` and embedded in the generated standalone HTML.

Upstream: https://github.com/mourner/flatqueue/tree/7e280d07ab202fc6494e16f07321ce3af60431ac

No CityFlow, OpenTTD, Generative Agents code or third-party game assets are distributed. The city planning, transport, production, technology and art implementations are original to this project. Research references and access limits are documented in `docs/RESEARCH_INTEGRATION.md` and `EVIDENCE/cities/research-sources.json`.

Realms 0.5 source research additionally inspected Unknown Horizons (GPL-2.0), Widelands (GPL-2.0), Citybound (AGPL-3.0) and ExoPlaSim's README/paper. Their code and assets are not distributed. See `docs/DEEP_RESEARCH.md` and `EVIDENCE/logistics/research-sources.json` for inspected sections, pinned revisions, hashes and integration decisions. The new production/hauling module is an original implementation.
