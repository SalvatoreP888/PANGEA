# Settlement and fantasy-world research — 17 October 2026

The useful combination for PANGAEA is WorldBox's readable living world, Songs of Syx's settlement scale, Foundation's organic layout, and a physical production economy. Realms 0.5 implements the next missing part: industrial inputs and outputs move through saved workshop inventories and actual residents. The commercial games are design references; their code, artwork, names and recipes are not imported.

This pass read nine games' official descriptions or documentation, inspected WorldBox's official screenshots and changelog, and examined three additional open-source city simulators. It was website/source research, not a hands-on playthrough of those games. Source URLs, response status and downloaded-file hashes are recorded in `EVIDENCE/logistics/research-sources.json`. Earlier blocked-page findings in `REALMS_RESEARCH.md` describe the previous environment; access now permits most primary sources.

## The implemented supply-chain change

Before 0.5, a miller reached a mill and immediately consumed grain from the town-wide store; flour appeared in that same store. The mill's position affected the worker's walk, but materials skipped the trip.

Now a resident reaches the common store, collects a bounded input shipment and carries it to a specific producer. The producer's saved input buffer must contain every ingredient before a worker can transform a batch. Finished goods stay in that producer's output buffer until collected and carried back to storage. A bakery cannot use flour still waiting in a mill. A train cannot export it either. At four batches of uncollected output, production stops. Perishables decay in workshop buffers as well as storage.

This applies to milling, baking, fish preparation, brick firing, weaving, iron, steel and machinery. Basic hearth cooking and primitive toolmaking retain the previous communal-input abstraction. The common store has no spatial capacity limit, and residents choose temporary hauling tasks rather than belonging to permanent logistics crews. Those boundaries are deliberate and visible in the implementation, not completed features.

Inventories, destination markers and completed-batch counts are saved. Interruptions, death and passenger departure preserve or recover cargo under the existing rules. Marked delivery saves reject incompatible cargo and invalid destinations. Rendering and inspectors read these states without creating inventories.

## Research papers and model limits

**[ExoPlaSim: Extending the Planet Simulator for Exoplanets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.07685), Paradise et al., arXiv:2107.07685v2.** Downloaded the complete PDF and read the abstract, introduction/model description, and performance section 3.1. This is a 3D general climate model with atmospheric layers, radiative processes and hydrology, rather than a latitude palette. The paper reports T21 at 32 latitudes × 64 longitudes and 10 vertical layers; its tested configurations simulate a year in tens of seconds on multiple MPI threads, while aquaplanet equilibration may need 50–200 model years. Those are the paper's measurements, not a PANGAEA benchmark. It supports an offline-reference approach, not a claim that the current temperature bands are physically complete. A practical next browser step is explicit wind/moisture transport and rain shadows, checked against qualitative climate patterns.

**[Elite Lanes: Evolutionary Generation of Realistic Small-Scale Road Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20964), arXiv:2603.20964v1.** Abstract inspected, not the full paper or implementation. Its reported connectivity, cycles, intersections, dead ends and cut-edge metrics are useful acceptance criteria for a later street revision. The current tree guarantees connectivity and removes the accidental lattice, but intentionally lacks redundant routes. An evolutionary generator has not been implemented or evaluated here.

**[CityFlow](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.05217), arXiv:1905.05217.** Abstract rechecked. A traffic-signal/reinforcement-learning platform addresses a different workload from this settlement economy. No traffic-signal or reinforcement-learning dependency is added.

An initially fetched arXiv identifier, 1709.09900, was unrelated astronomy research and was excluded. The broad hydrology query also returned an unrelated satellite-image paper. Fetching a search result is not evidence that it belongs in the design.

## Next work, with acceptance criteria

1. **District warehouses and last-mile routes.** Assign accepted goods, capacity and low/high targets; choose reachable sources by travel cost. Demonstrate grain arriving by train, delivery to a mill, flour reaching a bakery, and bread reaching residents without duplicated inventory. Closed routes and full storage must produce a clear, recoverable blocker. This builds on 0.5 rather than adding more unconnected resource names.
2. **Town catchments and geography.** Track water supply through the existing descending drainage graph; make drought and upstream use affect downstream farms and fisheries. Add wind and elevation barriers to global rainfall. Verify rain shadows, polar/equatorial gradients, bounded water use and old-save terrain preservation.
3. **Purposeful streets and city districts.** Keep homes near services, production near freight access and farms at the edge. Add only useful arterial connections/loops with a road-length budget. Compare travel cost, junction count, built frontage and disconnected doors across seeds; preserve the saturated-foot-traffic screenshot regression. Avoid returning to every empty parcel having four roads.
4. **Fantasy social observation.** Build inspectable local cultures, shared festivals and learned preferences from existing relationships and heritage. Every count must refer to actual people, and mixed families must remain possible. Keep species identity separate from compulsory profession assignment.
5. **Larger natural cities.** The laboratory proves constructed 250/1,000/2,000-person workloads, not natural growth to that scale. Run long multi-seed demographic and food-throughput checks before raising the cap or claiming naturally grown metropolises. Keep MAX responsive and replay exact at the measured tick count.

These are ordered research findings and future acceptance criteria. Only the producer-buffer/hauling improvement is newly implemented in 0.5.
